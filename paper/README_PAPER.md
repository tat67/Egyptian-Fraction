# Paper: integral reciprocal sums of distinct squarefree semiprimes

This directory contains the manuscript *"Integral sums of reciprocals of distinct squarefree semiprimes: forty-seven terms are necessary, and the extremal representations of 1"*. It also contains the scripts and logs of the verification work done for it.

The paper unifies the two results of this repository:

* **Theorem A** ([`../README.md`](../README.md)): a nonempty set of distinct squarefree semiprimes with an integral reciprocal sum has at least 47 elements, and 47 is attained.
* **Theorem B** ([`../README47.md`](../README47.md)): there are exactly 23 sets of 47 squarefree semiprimes whose reciprocal sum is 1.

Both theorems are **computer-assisted**. The Lean formalization proves them *conditionally* on the explicit hypotheses `Search46` and `Search47`, which encode the outcome of the exhaustive searches. See [`AUDIT_REPORT.md`](AUDIT_REPORT.md) for the precise status of every ingredient.

## Building the PDF

The manuscript needs TeX Live 2023 or later (pdfLaTeX, BibTeX, latexmk). It uses the packages amsmath/amsthm, mathtools, enumitem, booktabs, tabularx, algorithm/algpseudocode, TikZ, natbib, hyperref, cleveref, microtype and lmodern.

```
cd paper
latexmk -pdf main.tex        # runs pdflatex and bibtex as often as needed
```

The final build has no errors, no undefined references or citations, and no overfull or underfull boxes. The only BibTeX warning is the intentionally empty title of `Johnson1978` (the title could not be verified and is not invented). `latexmk` produces `main.pdf`; the committed copy is `paper.pdf`.

## Re-running the verification

```
cd paper/verification
python3 verify_paper_facts.py                     # ~5 s; exact checks and table generation
gunzip -c ../../cores47.txt.gz > /tmp/cores47.txt
python3 analyze47.py /tmp/cores47.txt 47 11       # ~30 s; Step-2 recount for budget 47
bash step1_bruteforce/run_bruteforce.sh           # ~2 min; Step-1 brute-force validation
python3 make_tables.py                             # regenerate the log-derived tables
```

The budget-46 re-execution takes about 20–25 minutes on 4 cores, and the budget-47 re-execution about 5.4 hours. The reproduction commands are listed in `verification/rerun/README.md` and `verification/rerun47/README.md`.
